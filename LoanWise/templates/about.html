What is an Expert System?

An Expert System uses human-engineered domain rules (stored in a Knowledge Base) and an Inference Engine to reason through complex financial data. Unlike pure ML models, expert rules guarantee strict compliance with hard constraints (such as legal age limits and negative disposable income overrides).

Why Combine Multiple ML Models?

Single machine learning models often suffer from bias or overfitting on specific feature distributions. By training Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Decision Tree, and Gradient Boosting simultaneously, LoanWise checks for model consensus and quantifies confidence levels.

Ensemble Decision Weighting

The final eligibility score (0–100) combines three distinct evaluation vectors:

  • 50% Machine Learning Ensemble Score: Performance-weighted probability average.
  • 30% Expert Rule Score: Normalized score based on domain risk criteria.
  • 20% Financial Health Score: Ratio analysis of DTI, EMI, and disposable income.
Explainable AI (XAI)

Traditional "black box" AI models output binary decisions without explanation. LoanWise explicitly reveals why a decision was reached, which specific rules were triggered, feature importances, and model agreement spreads.

Responsible AI & Ethical Lending Principles

No Protected Attribute Discrimination

LoanWise strictly excludes sensitive protected attributes such as race, religion, gender, ethnicity, or caste from all decision pipelines and model feature matrices.

Educational Prototype Notice

LoanWise is an educational prototype designed to demonstrate hybrid AI architectures. It does not issue actual banking lending decisions or credit guarantees.
